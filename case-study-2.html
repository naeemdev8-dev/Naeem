Challenge → Approach → Result

The challenge

Get conversations on a small budget

Daiichi Technologies wanted more people to message the business directly, while keeping spend under Rs900 for the flight.

The approach

A short, low-cost Messenger push

  • Promoted a Facebook page post with Messenger as the destination
  • Optimised for conversations with Meta's highest-volume bid strategy
  • Ran a compact three-day flight
  • Kept frequency at about 1.65 views per person
The result

Cheapest reach, efficient leads

  • 29 conversations for Rs886.12
  • Rs30.56 per conversation
  • Lowest CPM on the account (Rs233)
  • Above average on quality and engagement

Key takeaways

Quality and low cost go together

Above-average quality and engagement rankings went with the lowest CPM on the account.

Messenger beats traffic

Sending people straight to Messenger brought in conversations at about 1/27th of the traffic ad's cost.

Next lever: conversion

Conversion rate ranked average, so the next test is a sharper offer and a quick first reply to turn more viewers into chats.
