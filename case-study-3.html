Challenge → Approach → Result

The challenge

Find real buyers for an export vehicle

The client sells Japanese used cars to overseas buyers. It needed a steady flow of people interested in a specific vehicle, not just page views.

The approach

One vehicle, one clear message

  • Built the ad around a single in-demand model: the 2008 Toyota Hiace
  • Used the leads (conversions) objective so Meta looked for people likely to enquire
  • Bid with Meta's highest-volume strategy
  • Kept frequency at about 1.6 views per person to stay fresh
The result

Volume and quality together

  • 285 leads, the most of any ad on the account
  • Rs57.23 per lead
  • Nearly 40,000 people reached
  • Above-average quality ranking from Meta

Key takeaways

A specific vehicle sells the click

Built around one named model, the Hiace ad brought in more leads than all the other lead ads put together.

Quality keeps costs down

The ad with the above-average quality ranking was also the cheapest lead ad to deliver.

Scale what works

The smaller lead ads (England and Ireland, Uganda) cost Rs211 to Rs393 per lead. The Hiace ad kept costs at Rs57 per lead while scaling to almost 40,000 people.
