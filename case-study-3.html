Challenge → Approach → Result

The challenge

Find serious overseas buyers

The client sells Japanese used vehicles to buyers abroad. The goal was a steady flow of buyer enquiries for one specific vehicle, a 2008 Hiace, at a cost per lead that suits export margins.

The approach

One vehicle, one clear offer

  • Built the ad around one specific vehicle instead of a general showroom message
  • Used Meta's lead objective to collect buyer enquiries
  • Optimised for conversions with Meta's highest-volume bid strategy
  • Kept frequency at about 1.6 views per person
The result

Scale with quality

  • 285 leads from Rs16,310.76 of spend
  • Rs57.23 per lead
  • 39,686 people reached at a CPM of about Rs256
  • Above-average quality ranking from Meta

Key takeaways

Specific beats general

Advertising one clear vehicle gives buyers a concrete reason to enquire, which helps keep each lead cheap.

Quality keeps costs down

An above-average quality ranking helps an ad win delivery at a lower cost. Here the CPM stayed around Rs256.

Leads you can follow up

Each of the 285 leads is a buyer who raised their hand for this vehicle, so the sales team can follow up directly.
