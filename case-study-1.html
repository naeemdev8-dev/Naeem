Challenge → Approach → Result

The challenge

Turn page reach into real conversations

Daiichi Technologies needed people to start talking to the business, not just see a post. The goal was to get those conversations at the lowest possible cost.

The approach

Short, focused Messenger campaign

  • Promoted an existing Facebook page post with Messenger as the destination
  • Optimised for conversations with Meta's highest-volume bid strategy
  • Ran a tight five-day flight to test and learn quickly
  • Kept frequency to about 2 views per person to avoid ad fatigue
The result

Lowest cost, best quality

  • 64 conversations for Rs1,535.36
  • Rs23.99 per conversation, the best on the account
  • CPM of about Rs301
  • Above average on all three relevance rankings

Key takeaways

Send people to Messenger

Ads with Messenger as the destination brought in conversations for Rs24–31 each. The traffic-style ad cost Rs813.

Quality rankings track cost

Both ads Meta rated above average had the lowest cost per conversation on the account.

Keep improving

The second page-follow campaign got 1.9× the follows of the first (893 vs 464) at a 6% lower cost per follow.
